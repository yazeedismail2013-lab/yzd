Answering Common Questions

Learn how NFC cards, dynamic menu structures, and website setups work.

What is a Google Review NFC Card?
It is a smart card containing a minor NFC chip. When a client taps it with their smartphone, it immediately triggers the browser to open your Google business review page. It allows you to collect feedback in under 5 seconds.
Can I change my NFC card details after printing?
Yes. Because we use dynamic profile redirection systems, you can update your contact numbers, emails, portfolios, and social accounts in real-time without needing a new physical card.
Do you build custom websites or use WordPress templates?
We write custom HTML, CSS, and Javascript. This makes our sites significantly faster, cleaner, more secure, and perfectly tailored to luxury styling demands, resulting in better ranking and conversions.
How does the WhatsApp Purchase Flow work?
When you click "Purchase", you select the service you want and provide your Name, Mobile, and Location. Our website formats this instantly and directs you to a pre-filled WhatsApp message sent to Muhammed Yazeed C.P (+91 9400771619) for immediate project launch scheduling.
